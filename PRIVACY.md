# PlagX Writing Companion: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 18 November 2026_

PlagX Writing Companion ("the app") is published by Plagiarism Checker X.

## Summary

The app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. There are no accounts,
ads, analytics or tracking.

## Data on your device

Text you paste into Preflight, citations you create and notes you save are stored only on your device,
inside the app's own storage. They are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data
deletes them.

## Network requests

The app makes one kind of network request, and only when you use it: **DOI lookup** in Cite. When you
tap "Look up", the DOI you entered is sent over HTTPS to the public Crossref API
(`api.crossref.org`) to fetch the publication's details. No other data is sent. Crossref's own privacy
policy applies to that request: https://www.crossref.org/operations-and-sustainability/privacy/

The "Learn more" link on the About screen opens the Plagiarism Checker X website in your browser. The
app itself sends nothing when you tap it.

## Permissions

The app requests only Internet access, used for the DOI lookup above.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
